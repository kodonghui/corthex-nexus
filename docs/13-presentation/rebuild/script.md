# NEXUS usage demonstration

[Usage video](nexus-usage.mp4) · [한국어](script.ko.md)

For people using NEXUS for the first time. The 2-minute, 9-second walkthrough combines sample browser recordings and verified browser captures of the released interface. Korean narration, captions and video chapters guide viewers from the public home page through reading a project, asking a question and deciding a checkpoint. The sample conversation and decisions use synthetic data. [Production NEXUS](https://nexus.corthex-hq.com/) [Stage 12 QA](../../12-qa/rebuild/2026-11-19-rechecks.md)
