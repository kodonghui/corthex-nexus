[목업 진입 파일](https://github.com/kodonghui/corthex-nexus/blob/f4f765360a9ffdb32a5e8e5e934be1a7dcea3da9/design/rebuild/index.html)의 SHA-256은 `34731bfaf7b14733c28584a1f76b1f6839c628143252388e4d4af72bf820b79b`입니다.
