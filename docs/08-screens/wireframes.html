SCR-145#normal

The mockup's workbench: stage tabs under the header; the diagram or item table in the middle with dashed AI proposals and the legend; the work panel on the right with three sections. "This diagram": its state ("not confirmed", "confirmed", "N places changed since the confirmation") with up to four changes and "+N more" to SCR-146, "AI proposals left out are not confirmed", and the confirm button. "AI proposals": each with its kind, text, reason and the other way, and Accept and Reject ("All answered" when none). "Selected": the element's kind, name, its threads and the comment box with AI and owner badges. A never-confirmed diagram says nothing is marked (SPEC-034); a part of the document that cannot be read is left out with its file and line (SPEC-003). By stage: 03 draws each user flow as one diagram of its steps, decisions and labelled arrows, top to bottom as its Mermaid source says (SPEC-006); 08 draws one panel per state of a screen, with the state's name, its description and the responses that lead to it, and a state without a description is an empty panel marked "No description" (SPEC-008); 09 shows the mockup page in a frame isolated from NEXUS, its own links and tabs working inside it, and lists the design tokens it uses in the panel under "This diagram" (SPEC-009). Those stages have one diagram per file, listed above the diagram; choosing one opens it. A mockup's elements are its tokens, so a token is selected and commented on like any element; on a phone the token list follows the frame. The view reads the page's status before it frames it, and when the page opens another page in its frame it opens the mockup page again with "The mockup tried to open another page." On a phone: the diagram fills the width and the panel is a sheet under it with the threads and the comment box only; moving, renaming, connecting, cancelling edits, answering proposals, confirming and cancelling a confirmation are not offered, with "Do this on a PC" (SPEC-051).
