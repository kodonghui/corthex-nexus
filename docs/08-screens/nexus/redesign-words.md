# NEXUS: the redesign's words

[한국어](redesign-words.ko.md)

The words that the redesign's definitions, SCR-110 to SCR-117, need, drafted on 2026-11-07 for its checkpoint 3. A word between `·` marks is a label of its own; in Korean the agent is AI ([choice 17](README.md#choices-this-stage-made)). For the codes and states that 0.2 already has, the screens keep stage 08's [messages](README.md#messages). Since the redesign's build, `nexus/tools/messages.mjs` turns this file's table into the words of the redesign's views too, as it turns the tables of [README.md](README.md) into 0.2's ([Words the redesign added](README.md#words-the-redesign-added)), and it checks that the English and the Korean of each row hold as many labels with the same values in braces. So the two columns of words hold only the words; what a row means or where its words come from is in its Where column. When this file and its Korean translation differ, the English file is the source.
