# Run 2026-11-14 on the synthetic loopback server (commit dc2c22d)

[한국어](2026-11-14-release-walk.ko.md)

Before the go or no-go of NEXUS 0.3.0, TASK-314, four browser agents walked every screen that the release adds or changes, SCR-110 to SCR-115 and SCR-130, through FL-113, FL-114, FL-117 to FL-119 and FL-127 on the synthetic server, for TC-1786 and the release's cases that a browser takes. Of 22 rows, 20 work and 2 failed, with BUG-617 and BUG-614; the testers' notes held BUG-616 and BUG-618, and fixing BUG-614 showed BUG-615. All five are fixed in `b5c9705`, whose suites took the failed steps again in a browser, and every row is pinned in a suite of `nexus/test/browser_check.py` or a test of `node --test`.

- Tool: Playwright 1.63.0 with Chromium 153.0.8010.12, headless, on Node 24.22.1 · 4 testers at once, without sub-agents · 33 minutes, 17:38 to 18:11 KST
- Role: the maintainer, the one person who uses NEXUS, at a PC at 1,440 pixels and on a phone at 390 pixels
- Settings: each row in two of four settings, 390 pixels in light English and in dark Korean and 1,440 in light English and in dark Korean, each in a fresh browser in Seoul's time zone; taps at 390 pixels and clicks at 1,440; R/A1 and R/C3 also with the page's code held back
- Data: the fixture server `nexus/test/view-server.mjs` on loopback, one for each tester, with the first page's projects, the talk's, the item's, the agent loop's and the project's data (`NEXUS_QA_LOOP`, `NEXUS_QA_TALK`, `NEXUS_QA_ITEM`, `NEXUS_QA_DATA`), started again between rows that change its data. The fixture's acts stood in for the agent's push, its asking again, its replies and its read while a page was open, and cookies chose the sign-in, the person's own or the agent's service token. No real project, account or key took part

Why not ego lite or Aside: the session ran in a Linux cloud container. ego lite has no Linux version, and neither has Aside. Aside's remote control, through which `aside` on Linux drives Aside on another computer, was not turned on and approved with `aside login` for this session. The next walk can use ego lite from a session on the maintainer's computer where it is installed, with this repository and Node 24, or Aside once its remote control is on and `aside login` is approved.

The testers passed checkpoints, answered comments and removed only synthetic projects that they had seeded, since the walk tests those writes, and after each refusal they checked through the fixture's count that nothing was recorded.
