# Run 2026-11-13 on the synthetic loopback server (commit a6f963b)

[한국어](2026-11-13-group4-flows.ko.md)

Group 4 of the revision, TASK-295 to TASK-302, had four browser agents walk its screens on the synthetic server: the first page's checkpoint request and its pass (FL-117), the pass in the stage's record and its refusals (FL-113), and the loop's two times and the talk's marks (FL-108, FL-118, FL-119), for the revision's cases of the screens' states (TC-1704 to TC-1719, TC-1773). Of 19 rows, 16 work and 3 failed, with five bugs, BUG-606 to BUG-610. They are fixed in `8fbd75f`, whose suites took the failed steps again in a browser, and every row is pinned in a suite of `nexus/test/browser_check.py`.

- Tool: Playwright 1.63.0 with Chromium 153.0.8010.12, headless, on Node 24.22.1 · 4 testers at once, without sub-agents · 22 minutes, 21:41 to 22:03 KST
- Role: the maintainer, the one person who uses NEXUS, at a PC at 1,152, 1,280 and 1,440 pixels and on a phone at 390 pixels
- Settings: each row in the settings that it names, of 390 pixels in light English and in dark Korean, 1,152 in dark Korean, 1,280 in light English, and 1,440 in light English and in dark Korean, each in a fresh browser in Seoul's time zone; taps under 1,024 pixels and clicks from there; two rows also without the page's code
- Data: the fixture server `nexus/test/view-server.mjs` on loopback, one for each tester, started again between rows that change its data: G4/A with `NEXUS_QA_HOME=1`, `NEXUS_QA_PASS=1` and `NEXUS_QA_LOOP=1`, G4/B with `NEXUS_QA_HOME=1` and `NEXUS_QA_PASS=1`, G4/C with `NEXUS_QA_PASS=1` and `NEXUS_QA_STAGE=1`, and G4/D with `NEXUS_QA_LOOP=1` and `NEXUS_QA_TALK=1`. The fixture's acts stood in for the agent and its hub while a page was open: a push, the agent asking again, a new checkpoint request, a request's comment filled to its 200 replies, and the agent's read of its to-do. No real project, account or key took part

Why not ego lite or Aside: the session ran in a Linux cloud container. ego lite has no Linux version, and neither has Aside. Aside's remote control, through which `aside` on Linux drives Aside on another computer, was not turned on and approved with `aside login` for this session. The next walk can use ego lite from a session on the maintainer's computer where it is installed, with this repository and Node 24, or Aside once its remote control is on and `aside login` is approved.

The testers passed, withdrew, replied and answered on the fixture's synthetic projects only, since the walk tests those writes, and after each refusal they checked through the fixture's count that nothing was recorded.
