# Run 2026-11-13 on the synthetic loopback server (commit 97aef23)

[한국어](2026-11-13-redesign-flows.ko.md)

TASK-278 had browser agents walk the redesign's three flows, FL-117 to FL-119, on the pages that TASK-265 to TASK-277 built (TC-1785). Of 43 rows, 37 work, 2 failed and 4 are blocked. Both failures are fixed in `3f5cfd9` and were walked again in a browser, and the paths that no suite repeated are pinned in `nexus/test/flow-regression.mjs`.

- Tool: Playwright 1.63.0 with Chromium 153.0.8010.12, headless, on Node 24.22.1 · 3 testers at once, one per flow, without sub-agents · 34 minutes, 06:00 to 06:34 KST
- Role: the maintainer, the one person who uses NEXUS, at a PC at 1,440 pixels and on a phone at 390 pixels
- Settings: each row at 390 pixels in light English and in dark Korean and at 1,440 pixels in light English and in dark Korean, unless the row says otherwise; taps under 1,024 pixels and clicks above
- Data: the fixture server `nexus/test/view-server.mjs` on loopback, with the synthetic projects of the first page, the stage, the picture, the talk and the item; each tester started servers of its own, and no real project, account or key took part

Why not ego lite or Aside: the session ran in a Linux cloud container. ego lite has no Linux version, and neither has Aside. Aside's remote control, through which `aside` on Linux drives Aside on another computer, was not turned on and approved with `aside login` for this session. The next walk can use ego lite from a session on the maintainer's computer where it is installed, with this repository and Node 24, or Aside once its remote control is on and `aside login` is approved.

The brief named the fixture's projects as made for the walk, so the testers made the user's own presses, such as accepting a proposal or passing a checkpoint, only there (SPEC-200).
