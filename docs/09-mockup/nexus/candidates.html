Each item with entries gets tabs that sit on its top edge, above its label: one dark tab with an icon and a count for comments and for proposals, an amber tab for discrepancies, and a blue tab that says Accepted, not yet reflected. A tray of tabs under the picture holds Apart, the list of items and the legend. Panels first rise to half the height, without dimming, so that the item stays in sight, and show the talk as speech bubbles. Tokens토큰

In design/nexus/: base.tokens.json for type, space, radius, sizes and motion; theme/light.tokens.json and theme/dark.tokens.json for the colours and shadows of each theme, under the same names; tokens.resolver.json chooses between them. In the frames each token is a custom property, such as --nx-color-accent.design/nexus/에 있습니다. base.tokens.json은 글꼴, 간격, 모서리, 크기, 움직임이고, theme/light.tokens.json과 theme/dark.tokens.json은 테마마다의 색과 그림자를 같은 이름으로 담으며, tokens.resolver.json이 둘 중 하나를 고릅니다. 틀 안에서는 토큰마다 --nx-color-accent 같은 사용자 정의 속성이 됩니다.
